Age 116 vs. Age 115 — What's Actually Changing

A diff of the two Final Changes announcements. Mechanics that carried over unchanged from Age 115 are omitted — this covers only what moves for Age 116.

Overall Changes by Category

Buff / player-favorable Nerf / harsher New mechanic Structural / neutral

War Doctrine

2 changes
  • NerfPer-province doctrine growth cut for most races: +1.5% → +1% per additional province beyond the first.
  • BuffException carved out — Elf, Faery, and Halfling keep scaling at +2% per additional province, well above the new baseline.

Relations & Hostility

6 changes
  • NewUnfriendly/Hostile status (15/30 meter points) now unlocks a dedicated spell & thievery kit against that kingdom: Fireball, Lightning Strike, Fools Gold, Meteor Showers, Nightstrike, Greater Arson.
  • ReworkDFCF renamed to FCF and loosened: activation threshold drops from 45/30 → 30/15 meter points below the aggressor.
  • NerfFCF war-range penalty increased: 1.75% → 2% Honor & Science loss.
  • BuffFCF minimum duration extended: 48hr → 72hr (in-range) / 96hr (out-of-range) before either side can cancel.
  • ReworkHostile Meter decay reverts to pre-Age-111 rules: decays monthly by 20% or 3 points (whichever is greater), instead of ramping after 48hrs.
  • NerfHostile Meter no longer resets on Ceasefire or entering War — and Out-of-War attack gain penalty rises 10% → 15%.

Buildings & Science

4 changes
  • BuffWatch Tower spy-catch chance up: 2.0% → 2.2%.
  • BuffCunning and Sorcery science trees are merged back into one tree, Arcana, governing instant spell and thief damage.
  • BuffShielding multiplier raised 0.0314 → 0.0350; Valor multiplier raised 0.0582 → 0.0620.
  • NoteNo Castle base-percent or Resilience/Artisan/Finesse multiplier changes this age (those were 115-only shifts).

Spells

6 changes
  • BuffFireball peasant damage: 4–7% → 5–7%. Storms peasant loss/tick: 1.5% → 1.75%.
  • BuffDroughts strengthened — food reduction 25% → 30%, draft-rate reduction 15% → 20%.
  • BuffChastity, Sloth, and Magic Ward average duration shortened: 6 days → 4 days.
  • NerfLightning Strike pulled from global access — now race/personality-gated only (Necromancer retains it).
  • NoteNo new racial spell introduced this age (Age 115 added Soul Blight; 116 adds none).

Thievery

6 changes
  • BuffIncite Riots income hit: 15% → 20%.
  • BuffBribe Thieves TPA reduction: 10% → 12.5%.
  • BuffFree Prisoners damage: 17% → 25%.
  • BuffDestabilize Guilds duration reduction impact increased: 20% → 25%.
  • BuffArson damage: +5%.
  • BuffWar caps lifted — Rob the Vaults → 16%, Kidnap → 5%, opening more room for these ops during War.

Attacking & Combat

3 changes
  • NewIn War, successful Learn and Plunder attacks now also capture land — 20% of what a Traditional March would gain.
  • BuffMassacre peasant damage in War increased +5% (additive); Thief/Wizard damage unchanged.

Dragons

7 changes
  • BuffAmethyst hits harder: spell/thievery success reduction −30% → −40%; recurring wizard/thief kill 2% → 5%.
  • BuffEmerald arrival troop loss up: 1.5% → 3.5% (recurring tick loss unchanged at 1.5%).
  • BuffRuby arrival destroy up 2.5% → 3.5% and now hits both offensive and defensive specialists, not just offensive.
  • BuffTopaz building-efficiency debuff eased: −30% → −25%.
  • BuffSapphire rune destruction up 25% → 30% on arrival, and recurring tick shortened every 6 ticks → every 3 ticks.
  • NewDragons started outside of War now cost 15% more Gold and Food to summon.
  • NoteNo flat Dragon HP reduction this age (Age 115's −2% HP cut isn't repeated).
